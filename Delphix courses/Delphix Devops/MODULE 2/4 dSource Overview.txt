A dSource is a copy of the source database that
is compressed and stored in the Delphix Engine, 
along with all incremental updates, and is used to make virtual copies of your data. 

Linking dSources:
    A Linked dSource within the Delphix Continuous Data Engine will ingest data 
    from the Source Environment(s) and create a dSource object on the Delphix 
    Continuous Data Engine.

Enabling and Disabling dSources:
    Some operations, such as dSource upgrade, are not available unless the dSource is disabled. 
    Disabling a dSource turns off communication between the Delphix Engine and the source database, 
    but it does not remove the configuration.

    When a disabled dSource is later enabled, it will resume communication and incremental data updates 
    from the source database according to the original policies and data management configurations.

In dashboard,
Step 1 - Find the Datasets Menu
step 2 - Choose a dSource to Disable
step 3 - Open the dSource Options
step 4 - confirm the Disable action
Step 5 - Review the status of the dsource

Disabling a dSource is also a prerequisite for several other operations, such as: 

1. Database migration
2. Upgrading the dSource metadata after the upgrade of the associated data source
3. Restoring the source database from a backup
